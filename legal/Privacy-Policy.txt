FUNNEL — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Applies to: Funnel for Windows, version 1.0.0 and later

Funnel ("the app") is a desktop application for company research, peer
comparisons, discounted-cash-flow modelling and Excel export. This policy
explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you
have. In short: Funnel runs entirely on your computer, has no user accounts,
and does not send any information to its developer.


1. WHO WE ARE

Funnel is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). Questions about this
policy can be sent to [CONTACT EMAIL].


2. THE SHORT VERSION

  - No accounts or sign-in. Funnel opens in guest mode only.
  - No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  - We do not operate a server. Nothing you do in Funnel is sent to us.
  - Your settings, saved comp sets and DCF cases stay on your computer.
  - To fetch market data, Funnel connects directly from your computer to the
    third-party data sources you choose (listed in section 5). Those services
    see the requests you make, as described below.


3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR COMPUTER

Funnel keeps the following in its own folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Funnel:

  a) Settings file (config.json), containing:
       - your preferred data source for each section of the app;
       - display preferences (units, annual/quarterly, macro panel on/off);
       - your Excel export folder;
       - recently viewed ticker symbols;
       - peer sets you have saved for each ticker;
       - DCF model assumptions you have saved for each ticker;
       - the contact email you entered for SEC EDGAR, if any;
       - API keys you entered for optional data providers, if any.

     API keys are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so they can
     only be decrypted by your Windows user account on that computer. The
     other settings, including the SEC contact email, are stored as plain text.

  b) Web view data (the WebView2 subfolder). The app's interface runs in
     Microsoft Edge WebView2. That component keeps its usual cache and
     browser storage in this folder. Funnel loads its interface from files
     built into the app and does not load outside web pages, fonts or scripts
     into it.

  c) Excel workbooks you export. These are saved where you choose (by default
     Documents\Funnel) and contain only the market data and model inputs you
     selected for export.

  d) Crash log. If the app crashes, it writes a file named Funnel-crash.log to
     your Desktop (or, failing that, your temporary folder or the app folder)
     and shows it on screen. The log contains the time and technical error
     details, which may include file paths on your computer such as your
     Windows user name. The log is never sent anywhere; you choose whether to
     share it.

None of this information is transmitted to us.


4. INFORMATION SENT OVER THE INTERNET

Funnel needs an internet connection to retrieve market data. When you look up
a company, open a page, or refresh data, the app sends requests directly from
your computer to the relevant data source. As with any internet request, the
receiving service can see:

  - your IP address and the time of the request;
  - what you asked for, such as ticker symbols, search text, statement type,
    date ranges and chart intervals;
  - a software identifier (user-agent string).

In addition, depending on the source:

  - SEC EDGAR: the SEC requires every automated client to identify itself.
    If you choose SEC EDGAR as a source, Funnel includes the contact email
    you entered in each request to the SEC (in the User-Agent header). Funnel
    does not contact the SEC on your behalf until you provide this email.

  - Financial Modeling Prep and Finnhub: if you enable these optional
    providers, the API key you entered is sent with each request to that
    provider, as their service requires.

  - Yahoo Finance: to make requests, Funnel obtains a session cookie and
    access token from Yahoo. These are kept in memory only while the app is
    running and are not saved to disk.

Funnel does not send your settings, saved comp sets, DCF models, exported
files or crash logs to any data source.


5. THIRD-PARTY DATA SOURCES

Depending on your choices in the Data sources panel, Funnel may contact:

  - Yahoo Finance (query1/query2.finance.yahoo.com, fc.yahoo.com)
      Default source for quotes, charts, statements, comps, analysts, news.
  - U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission — EDGAR
      (www.sec.gov, data.sec.gov, efts.sec.gov)
      Financial statements as filed, industry peers and filings.
  - Financial Modeling Prep (financialmodelingprep.com) — optional, API key.
  - Finnhub (finnhub.io) — optional, API key.
  - Bank of Canada (www.bankofcanada.ca) — interest rates and exchange rates.
  - U.S. Department of the Treasury (home.treasury.gov) — Treasury yield curve.

Each of these services handles the requests it receives under its own privacy
policy and terms of use, which we do not control. We encourage you to review
them, especially for any provider where you have created an account to obtain
an API key.

If a source you selected cannot answer a request, Funnel falls back to Yahoo
Finance for that request and tells you once when this happens.

External links: some screens show links such as a company's website. Clicking
one opens it in your default web browser, where that site's own policies
apply.

Microsoft WebView2: the app's interface is displayed by the Microsoft Edge
WebView2 Runtime installed on your computer. Microsoft may collect diagnostic
data from that runtime according to your Windows and Microsoft Edge settings
and Microsoft's privacy statement. Funnel turns off developer tools and the
default context menus in its window but does not change Microsoft's
diagnostic settings.


6. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information stored by Funnel is used only to run the app on your computer:
to remember your preferences, restore your saved peer sets and models, route
requests to the sources you chose, and authenticate with optional providers.
We do not sell, rent, share or otherwise disclose your information, because we
never receive it.


7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Everything Funnel stores stays until you remove it. You can:

  - remove an API key or change your SEC contact email in the Data sources
    panel;
  - delete exported workbooks and crash logs like any other file;
  - delete all app data by closing Funnel and deleting the folder
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Funnel. This removes your settings, saved peer sets, saved
    DCF cases, encrypted API keys and web view data.

Uninstalling or deleting Funnel.exe alone does not remove that folder.

Information already sent to a third-party data source is held by that
provider under its own policy. Contact the provider directly about it.


8. SECURITY

API keys are encrypted at rest using Windows DPAPI and tied to your Windows
user account. All connections to data sources use HTTPS. Because other
settings are stored as plain text in your user profile, anyone with access
to your Windows account can read them. Protect your account with a password
and keep your computer secure.


9. CHILDREN

Funnel is a financial research tool intended for adults and students. It is
not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your
jurisdiction), and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.


10. YOUR RIGHTS

PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have rights to access,

Funnel stores your information only on your own computer and we hold none of
it, you can exercise these rights directly using the steps in section 7. For
information held by a third-party data source, contact that provider. For any

11. NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE

Market data, analyst figures and model outputs in Funnel come from third
